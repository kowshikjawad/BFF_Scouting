## Current design direction

The 2026-10-28 completion specification supersedes the earlier dark broadcast-screen concept.
Use the minimal light scouting workspace documented in `DESIGN.md` and
`specs/001-complete-scouting-workspace/spec.md`.

White panels, cool off-white shell, deep pitch green actions, restrained gold/red statuses.
Inter UI and Space Grotesk titles, small radii, thin borders, desktop sidebar and mobile menu.
Table and card views support discovery. All visual assets are open-source and bundled locally;
license notices ship in `frontend/public/licenses/`.

Real player records use initials avatars. Missing tactical values remain unknown. Radar comparisons
need complete same-league/position cohorts. Eligibility screening explicitly identifies its limits.
Final verification is recorded in `specs/001-complete-scouting-workspace/verification.md`.
